Taniti: Where Adventure Awaits

Welcome to Taniti, a tropical paradise with something for everyone!

Whether you're seeking relaxation on pristine beaches, thrilling outdoor adventures, or a taste of local culture and cuisine, Taniti has it all. Explore our diverse accommodations, indulge in delicious dining options, and discover the many activities that make Taniti a must-visit destination.

Most visitors come during our dry season from May to October, when it is sunny, less humid, and there is usually a comfortable breeze.

In July you can experience our Time of Plenty Festival!
